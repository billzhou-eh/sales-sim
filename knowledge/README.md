## The one rule that matters most
A missing *document* never physically stops a process. What stops or jeopardizes
production is a physical fault (trip, off-spec, under-processing) or a quality/compliance
*decision* (hold, deviation, permit exceedance). Documents are evidence, not the hazard.
And instruments ship pre-calibrated — customers recalibrate, verify, and qualify; they
never "certify."
